Not aspirations – the small set of rules I return to when a decision is hard. Fewer, but kept.

## The list

1. **Default to writing.** If it's worth thinking about, it's worth a note. That's why this garden exists.
2. **Ship the rough draft.** [[agent-orchestration]] beats polishing in private.
3. **Own my tools.** Prefer plain text and things I can carry between apps for decades.
4. **Compound, don't chase.** Small, linked ideas grow into something; scattered ones don't.
5. **Strong opinions, loosely held.** Update fast when the evidence changes.

These sit underneath most of what I write in [[Concepts]].
