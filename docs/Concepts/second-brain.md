A **second brain** is an external, trusted system for the things your biological one is bad at: remembering, connecting, and not losing ideas. The point isn't to hoard notes – it's to free your head for the work only it can do.

## What makes it work

- **Capture is frictionless.** If saving a thought takes effort, you won't.
- **Notes link.** A note in isolation is a dead end; the value is in the connections. This is what separates a [[digital-garden]] from a folder of documents.
- **You revisit.** A second brain you never reopen is just a graveyard.

## How I run mine

I keep everything as plain-text Markdown in [[obsidian]], then publish the parts worth sharing here. Writing in the open – [[learning-in-public]] – keeps me honest about what I actually understand.
