A digital garden is a website you tend rather than publish. Instead of finished, dated blog posts, it's a web of notes that grow, link to each other, and change over time.

## Why it works
- **Low pressure.** Notes can be rough. Nothing has to be "done."
- **Compounding.** Ideas link together and get richer as the garden grows.
- **For yourself first.** You write to think, and share as a side effect.

This site is built on a small static generator that turns a folder of Markdown into linked pages – including Obsidian-style `[[wikilinks]]`.
