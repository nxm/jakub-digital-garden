My daily drivers are NixOS and macOS. I usually connect to the NixOS machine over SSH and tunnel whatever services I'm developing at the moment.

## Fundamentals

- **Helium** – my browser; Chromium-based, minimal and fast.
- **Neovim** – my editor and IDE, tuned with a custom config. Where most of the work happens.
- **Audible** – audiobooks for walks and commutes.
- **Goodreads** – tracking what I've read and what's next.
- **Kindle** – e-book reader (jailbroken, running KOReader for extra formats and freedom).

## Productivity

- **Plane** – open-source project and task management; issues, cycles, and roadmaps.
- **Obsidian** – my [[second-brain]]: a markdown vault with a few plugins, and the engine behind this garden.
- **Superhuman** – fast, keyboard-driven email client.

## Communication

- **AyuGram** – Telegram fork with extra privacy and quality-of-life tweaks.
- **Vesktop** – lightweight Discord fork; better performance, less bloat.
- **Signal** – private messaging, the default for anything sensitive.
- **WhatsApp** – family and everyday contacts.
- **Slack** – work and community channels.

## Automation

- **Nix scripts** – declarative config; my machines rebuild from code, not clicks.
- **Apple Shortcuts** – small automations gluing iOS and macOS together.
- **BetterTouchTool** – custom gestures, window snapping, and keyboard remaps.
- **WisprFlow** – voice-to-text dictation for fast capture.
- **Tailscale** – private mesh VPN; reach my machines from anywhere.
- **Secretive** – SSH keys stored in the Secure Enclave.
- **Bitwarden** – password manager.
- **Lens** – Kubernetes cluster observability, a GUI over `kubectl`.

## Creativity

- **ScreenFlow** – how I record and edit screen captures.
- **iA Writer** – distraction-free Markdown writing.
