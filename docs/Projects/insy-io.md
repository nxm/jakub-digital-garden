A marketplace where digital creators turn what they know into income – and a new way to get that work in front of people. Two halves that feed each other: a **marketplace** for selling knowledge, and **clipping** for spreading it.

## The marketplace

One place for a creator to package and sell what they know:

- **Paid communities** – recurring access to a private group.
- **Digital products** – ebooks, templates, downloads.
- **Consultations** – booked 1:1 time.
- **Video & courses** – standalone videos and full module courses.

Creators keep the audience relationship; insy handles payments, access, and delivery.

## Clipping

A new promotion channel. **Clippers** – accounts with reach – cut and post a creator's content, and the creator **pays per view**.

- **Performance-based** – budget goes to views that land, not flat sponsorship fees.
- **Distributed reach** – many small and mid accounts instead of one big ad buy.
- **Aligned incentives** – clippers earn more when the clips perform.

An open, measurable affiliate layer for attention.

## Who it's for

Creators sitting on knowledge – people [[agent-orchestration]] with a [[second-brain]] worth sharing – who want to monetize without stitching together five separate tools. Same spirit as the [[digital-garden]]: own your work, publish it, let it compound.

## Why

The creator economy is fragmented – one tool for community, another for courses, another for payments, another for promotion. insy.io folds monetization and distribution into a single loop: sell in the marketplace, amplify through clipping, reinvest the revenue into more clips.

## Status

Concept / early sketch. Shaping the model before building.
