My gym split – Push / Pull / Legs – run alongside the [[Training]] system (gym for strength, running for conditioning). Progress by heavier load or a harder variation – not just more reps. Per-set reps land once the weights settle; for now I track working sets (`× sets`).

## Push – chest, shoulders, triceps

- [ ] Push-ups (pompki): 15 × 3
- [ ] Flat bench press (ławka płaska): × 4
- [ ] Shoulder press (wyciskanie na barki): × 4
- [ ] Incline press (skos): × 4
- [ ] Cable crossover (brama): × 4
- [ ] Machine press (maszyna): × 3
- [ ] Shoulders–triceps–biceps (barki–triceps–biceps): × 3

## Pull – back, rear delts, biceps

- [ ] TRX rows: × 3
- [ ] Lat pulldown to chest (przyciąganie do klatki, wyciąg górny): × 4
- [ ] Deadlift (martwy): × 4
- [ ] Barbell row (przyciąganie sztangi): × 4
- [ ] Straight-arm pulldown / "narciarz" (wyciąg): × 3
- [ ] Behind-the-neck pulldown (wyciąg za głowę): × 3
- [ ] Seated cable row (wyciąg do klatki poziomo): –
- [ ] Biceps–shoulders–triceps (biceps–barki–triceps): –
- [ ] Pull-ups (podciąganie): –

## Legs – quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves

- [ ] Squat (przysiad): × 4
- [ ] Leg press (wyciskanie nogami): × 4
- [ ] Lunges (wykroki): × 3 per leg
- [ ] Leg curl (uginanie nóg leżąc): × 3
- [ ] Leg extension (prostowanie nóg): × 3
- [ ] Calf raises (łydki): × 4

Deadlift lives on the Pull day, so this day leans quads and single-leg work.

## Core – add to any day

- [ ] Plank (deska): × 3
- [ ] Hanging leg raise (unoszenie nóg w zwisie): × 3
- [ ] Cable crunch (spięcia na wyciągu): × 3
- [ ] Pallof press (anti-rotation): × 3 per side

## Notes

- Exercise names kept in my own gym shorthand (brama, maszyna, narciarz) with an English gloss.
- Sets logged as `× N`; per-set reps to be filled in once loads stabilise.
