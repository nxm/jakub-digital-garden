An all-in-one scraper across the marketplaces where things get bought and sold – OLX, Vinted, Allegro, Otomoto, Otodom, and many more – in one place. Set what you're hunting for, and get a **notification the moment it's listed**.

## What it does

- **One search, every platform** – define a query once; snipeit watches all connected marketplaces.
- **Instant alerts** – ping the second a matching listing appears, before the crowd sees it.
- **Filters that matter** – price, location, condition, keywords – so the alert is a real hit, not noise.

## Sources

Aggregates listings from marketplaces like:

- **OLX** – general second-hand.
- **Vinted** – fashion and collectibles.
- **Allegro** – broad marketplace and auctions.
- **Otomoto** – cars and vehicles.
- **Otodom** – real estate.
- ...and more, added over time.

## Who it's for

Collectors who compete for the same items – where the person who reacts **first** wins. When a rare piece drops at a good price, the difference between getting it and missing it is measured in seconds, not hours. snipeit turns that reaction time into an edge.

## Why

Manually refreshing five marketplaces doesn't scale, and the good listings are gone before you finish scrolling. One aggregated feed with instant alerts collapses the search-and-refresh loop into a single push notification.

## Open questions

- **Scraping resilience** – platforms change layouts and rate-limit; keeping sources alive is the core cost.
- **Alert latency** – how fast is fast enough, and what pipeline gets there.
- **Terms of service** – staying on the right side of each platform's rules.

## Status

Concept / early sketch. Validating the platforms and the alerting pipeline first.
