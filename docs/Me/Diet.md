This note is the control panel for how I eat by default. Daily details live in the log below. It should stay simple enough to scan when I'm tired, hungry, or writing a shopping list.

## Purpose

Eat in a way that supports [[Training]], recovery, energy, and hunger control – without making food complicated.

## Constraints

- **Fish allergy** – no fish or seafood anywhere. Cover omega-3 from plants: walnuts, ground flax, chia (or a supplement).

## Direction

Recomposition, leaning slightly toward fat loss: hold or add muscle, drop modest fat. A small deficit, not a punishing one. One morning weigh-in a week is the only number I track.

| Priority | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| High protein | Every bigger meal has a clear protein source. |
| Enough energy | Carbs are useful, especially around training. |
| Fresh food | Add fruit or vegetables daily – the easiest part to miss. |
| Low friction | Meals should be easy to prepare and realistic to repeat. |
| Repeatable | Keep the same useful foods around instead of reinventing dinner nightly. |

## Meal system

Most meals follow this shape:

| Part | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Protein | skyr, Greek yogurt, kefir, cottage cheese, eggs, lean meat, chicken, tofu, lentils, chickpeas |
| Energy | potatoes, rice, oats, banana, berries, wholegrain bread |
| Fresh | cucumber, tomato, spinach, asparagus, broccoli, cauliflower, berries |
| Fat | avocado, olive oil, nuts, seeds (walnuts / flax / chia for omega-3) |
| Simple prep | smoothie, eggs + potatoes, meat + vegetables |

## Default meals

| Meal | Default | Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breakfast smoothie | skyr + banana + berries + cocoa + nuts | banana + skyr |
| Training smoothie | skyr + fruit + cocoa + optional protein powder | skyr + banana + powder |
| Easy dinner | protein + potatoes/rice + vegetables + olive oil/avocado | eggs + potatoes + cucumber |
| Light supper | kefir or skyr + fruit + cottage cheese | kefir + banana |

## Operating rules

- Keep meals simple enough to **repeat** – a good meal I repeat beats a perfect one I avoid.
- Prioritize protein in the bigger meals.
- Use carbs deliberately, mostly around training.
- Add something fresh when I can.
- Prefer a filling meal over random snacking.
- A good smoothie beats skipping food or eating nonsense.
- **Anchor every bigger meal with protein** – even a pasta dinner (add eggs, cottage cheese, lentils, or meat).
- **Include a fat source daily** – avocado, olive oil, nuts, seeds.
- Aim for roughly **1.6–2.2 g protein per kg bodyweight**; a palm-sized protein serving per main meal is a good proxy.
- Cap smoothies at one a day – chew the rest for better fullness in a deficit.

## The log

What I eat is logged as a section of the day's note rather than a note of its own. Nothing ever links to "the diet of 17 August" as a unit, so a file per day per pillar would be three notes where one will do – and the date already identifies the day.

Training is the exception: a session has substance worth comparing across weeks, so it keeps its own note under `Me/Training/`. That asymmetry is deliberate.

The daily log itself stays out of the published garden.
