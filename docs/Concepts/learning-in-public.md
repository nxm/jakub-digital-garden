Most learning happens in private and stays there. **Learning in public** is the choice to put the messy middle out where others can see it – notes, questions, half-wrong takes, and all.

## Why it compounds

- **Writing forces clarity.** You can't hand-wave to an audience the way you can in your own head.
- **Feedback finds you.** Publish a wrong idea and someone will correct it faster than you'd find it alone.
- **It builds a trail.** Each note is a breadcrumb others (and future-me) can follow – the whole premise of a [[digital-garden]].

## The catch

It only works if you lower the bar for "done." Perfectionism is the enemy here; see [[principles]]. Publish the rough draft, keep tending it, and let the [[second-brain]] accumulate.
